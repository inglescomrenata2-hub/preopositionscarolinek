Preposicao no final.

Speaking com preposicoes deslocadas ao final da frase e vocabulario de personal brand. Contexto: marketing corporativo, JDE Peet’s.

What is she known for? What does she align her work with? What does she differentiate herself by?

O padrao

Como funciona: preposicao fica no final da pergunta ou da frase

Nas collocations de personal brand, a preposicao faz parte da estrutura do verbo. Quando essa expressao vira uma pergunta ou uma frase relativa, a preposicao vai para o final — ela nao sai da frase.

Padrao pergunta:  What / Who + aux + sujeito + verbo prep?

Padrao frase relativa:  “That is what/who + sujeito + verbo prep.”

Exemplos por collocation

Pergunta → Resposta com preposicao no final

What does she communicate her value by?
→
Sharing clear results is what she communicates her value by.
What is her reputation shaped through?
→
Consistent delivery is what her reputation is shaped through.
What does she align her work with?
→
Brand strategy is what she aligns her work with.
What does she build trust by?
→
Keeping promises is what she builds trust by.
Who does she cultivate connections with?
→
Key stakeholders are who she cultivates connections with.
What does she differentiate herself by?
→
Her cross-brand thinking is what she differentiates herself by.
What is she known for?
→
Her portfolio expertise is what she is known for.
What does she establish her brand by?
→
Her track record is what she establishes her brand by.

Regra de ouro

  • A preposicao pertence ao verbo — ela nao pode desaparecer. That is what she is known. ← ERRADO. Falta for.
  • Perguntas informais: a preposicao vai pro final. What is she known for? ← natural.
  • Respostas com frase relativa: “That is what / who…” + preposicao no final.

Warm-up — 30 segundos

  • Leia a pergunta em voz alta primeiro — note a preposicao no final.
  • Responda usando o padrao: “___ is what I ___ [prep].”
  • Tente estender a resposta com pelo menos mais uma frase.
